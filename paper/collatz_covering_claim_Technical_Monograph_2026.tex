\begin{abstract}
Standalone Lean 4 formalization (`CoveringClaim.lean`, `CoveringClaim/Claim.lean`, `CoveringClaim/Defs.lean`) giving a clean, type-checked formal specification of the open Collatz covering claim over residue classes modulo powers of two.
\end{abstract}

\section{Scope, Scientific Honesty, and Non-Claim Boundary}
Formalizes the exact mathematical statement of the open Collatz covering claim in Lean 4 without claiming a proof of the Collatz conjecture.

This repository contains one equation: every positive integer reaches 1.

```lean

def RemainingLemma : Prop :=

∀ n : Nat, 0 < n → ∃ k : Nat, iter k n = 1

axiom remaining\_lemma\_assumption : RemainingLemma

```

That axiom is not a proof. The Terras density equation is not in this repository. It lives in `collatz-terras-density`. A first descent is not arrival at 1.
